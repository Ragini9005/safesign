# 🛡️ SafeSign AI Auditor

### AI-Powered Terms & Privacy Risk Analyzer

SafeSign AI Auditor is a Chrome extension that uses **Gemini AI** to analyze Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy pages and highlight potentially risky clauses and hidden conditions.

## 🚨 Problem

Users often accept legal documents without reading them because they are:

* Long and difficult to understand
* Filled with complicated legal language
* Likely to contain hidden conditions
* Difficult to analyze for data collection and retention

## 💡 Solution

SafeSign provides a quick AI-powered analysis of the current legal page.

### Key Features

* 🛡️ Consumer protection score
* ⚠️ Hidden trap detection
* 🔍 Risk indicators
* ⏱️ Estimated reading time
* 📊 Data and privacy insights
* 💡 Action recommendation

## 🧠 How It Works

```text
Terms / Privacy Page
        ↓
SafeSign Extension
        ↓
Extract Legal Text
        ↓
Gemini AI Analysis
        ↓
Risk & Trap Detection
        ↓
Simple Dashboard
```
